## What it does

The phrase bank holds sixty ready-made phrases in twelve categories. Seven are general: opening, empathy, hold and waiting, verification, transfer and escalation, closing, and de-escalation. Five were written for travel and lodging work: booking details, cancellations and refunds, property issues, calling a property, and agencies and partners. Pick a category, click a phrase and it is copied to your clipboard, ready to paste into a chat or an email.

## How to use it

1. Choose a category from the two rows of buttons. The general categories are in the first row and the lodging and B2B categories in the second.
2. Read the phrases. Each one is a full button, so click anywhere on it to copy.
3. Paste the phrase, then replace anything in [square brackets] with your own details, such as the company name, a date or a reference number.
4. Adjust the wording until it sounds like you. A phrase that you read aloud word for word is easy to hear.

## Worked example

A guest calls to cancel a booking and the free-cancellation deadline has passed. In **Lodging: cancellations and refunds** you copy "That deadline has passed, so the cancellation policy applies: [penalty]. I can ask the property to review it, but I can't promise the outcome." You replace the bracket with "the first night is charged", and you have said what the policy is, what you can do and what you cannot promise, in two sentences.

In **B2B: calling a property** the second phrase asks the hotel to read you the confirmation number they hold. That one question catches most mismatches between what you booked and what the hotel has, before the guest arrives.

## Why the brackets matter

Every phrase that needs a fact has a bracket where the fact goes, so it is hard to read out a phrase that still says "[date]". The brackets also mark the places where your account has its own approved wording. Where your company provides scripts or compliance statements, use those. The phrases here are starting points that avoid promises, avoid blame and say what happens next.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Reading phrases flat.** The same sentence sounds warm or cold depending on how you say it. Adapt the words to the person.
- **Promising what you cannot control.** "I'll ask the property to review it" is honest. "They will refund you" is a promise someone else has to keep.
- **Skipping the recap.** Several phrases end by repeating what was agreed. Customers and partners rely on that, and so does the note you write afterwards.
- **Pasting without checking brackets.** Search for "[" before you send.

## Questions

::: faq
Q: Can I add my own phrases?
A: Not in this version. The list is fixed so every visitor sees the same sixty phrases.
Q: Are these approved scripts?
A: No. They are general examples. Your account's approved scripts and compliance wording always come first.
Q: Why did nothing happen when I clicked?
A: The page needs permission to use the clipboard, and some browsers block it on insecure pages. A "Phrase copied" message appears when it works, and the tool tries a second method if the first is blocked.
:::
