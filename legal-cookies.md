## What Agent Desk stores

The site uses your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies, to remember your own settings and work. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy.html) lists each key. In summary: your notes, your handle-time target, your case-note drafts, whether reports load automatically, whether focus mode is on, and your chosen theme. These items are needed for the features you use and for remembering your choices.

## What the site does not use

The site contains no analytics, no advertising pixels of its own and no social-media tracking widgets. Fonts are hosted on this site, so loading a page does not contact a font provider.

## Cookies from outside services

**Microsoft Power BI.** When a report loads, Microsoft may set cookies or use similar technologies as part of delivering the report. Reports load only when you press the button, when you choose to load them automatically, or when you open one in the workspace. Microsoft's privacy statement describes what it collects.

**Google AdSense.** Where advertising is shown, Google and its advertising partners may set cookies and use similar technologies to serve ads, limit how often you see them and measure their performance. Ads that use cookies or personal data in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown only after you have had the chance to consent through a consent message. Ads are never shown in the workspace or on the legal, contact and about pages.

**Hosting.** The host may use technical cookies or server logs that are necessary to deliver the site securely.

## Managing cookies and stored data

Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies and site data. Blocking local storage does not stop the tools from working, but they will not remember your notes or settings between visits, and they will tell you so. You can also clear a tool's saved data with its own "Clear" button. To control personalised advertising from Google, visit [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Changes

We will update the date at the top of this page if the cookies or storage described here change.
