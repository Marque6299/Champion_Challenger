## Who runs this site

Agent Desk is an independent site. You can reach its author through the [contact page](/contact/). This policy describes the site at the address you are reading it on.

## What the tools do with what you type

Everything you enter into a tool is processed in your browser. It is not sent to our servers, and the site has no accounts, forms that submit data, or database. If you close the page, anything not saved on your device is gone.

## What is saved on your device

Some tools remember your work so that a refresh does not lose it. They use your browser's local storage, which stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time from your browser's settings, or with the "Clear" buttons in the tools.

| Key | Used by | What it holds |
|-----|---------|---------------|
| `agentNotes` | Call Scratchpad | The text of your notes |
| `ahtTarget` | AHT Timer, AHT Calculator | Your handle-time target in seconds |
| `lodgingCaseType`, `lodgingDraft:` followed by a case type | Lodging Case Note Builder | The case type you chose and the draft fields for each type |
| `reportAutoload` | Report pages | Whether to load Power BI reports automatically |
| `focusMode` | Workspace | Whether focus mode is on |
| `theme` | All pages | Your choice of System, Dark or Light |

Do not type card numbers, security codes, passwords or full identity numbers into any note. The notes tools warn you when something looks like a card number, but that check is a safety net, not a guarantee.

## What we do not do

The site does not include analytics or tracking scripts of its own. It does not build profiles of visitors, sell data, or ask you to create an account. Fonts are served from this site, so your browser does not contact a font service when a page loads.

## Outside services

**Hosting.** The site is served by a hosting provider, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as the time of a request, the page requested, your IP address and your browser's identification string. That is how the page reaches you, and we do not use those logs to identify visitors.

**Microsoft Power BI.** Report pages and the workspace can show Power BI reports. A report loads from Microsoft's servers only when you ask for it, or when you have chosen to load reports automatically. Microsoft may set cookies or collect data when a report loads. Its privacy statement applies to that content.

**Google AdSense.** The site is applying to show advertising through Google AdSense. Where ads are shown, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, which can include ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised advertising at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com), and Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Ads are never shown in the workspace or on the legal, contact and about pages. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent message is shown, where required, before ads that use cookies or personal data.

## Your choices

You can clear stored data in your browser, block cookies, or use your browser's privacy settings. Because the site does not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to look up, correct or delete; if you believe we hold something about you, write to us and we will tell you what we hold and remove it where we can. For data held by Google or Microsoft, use their own privacy controls.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be described on this page.
