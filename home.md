## What you will find here

**Reports.** Three Power BI reports open inside the workspace or on their own pages. Each page explains what the report is for and how to read it, so a new reader does not have to guess what a chart means.

**Tools.** Seven everyday tools cover the small jobs of a shift: timing a call, writing notes, spelling an email address, converting a callback time. Eight more were built for travel and lodging work, where agents answer guests and call hotels, agencies and partners. They calculate cancellation deadlines in the hotel's own time zone, price changes, commission and net rates, group blocks and rooming lists, partner response times and hotel performance measures, and they build structured case notes.

**Guides and a glossary.** The guides explain the thinking behind the tools: how handle time is made up, how cancellation policies are worded, how to run a call with a hotel or an agency, and how to write notes the next agent can use. The glossary defines the terms you meet on the job.

## How it treats your data

The tools run in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server, and nothing needs an account. Notes and drafts are kept on your own device so they survive a refresh, and the tools warn you if a note looks as though it contains a card number or a security code. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy.html) lists exactly what is stored and which outside services are involved.
