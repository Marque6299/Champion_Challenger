Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

At Agent Desk, available at overwatchcc.netlify.app, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Agent Desk and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.

1. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

Mandatory AdSense Disclosure:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Agent Desk follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this as a part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps
  • Referring / exit pages
  • Number of clicks

These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Agent Desk itself does not set cookies. The tools save two things in your browser's local storage so they can remember them on your device: your call notes and your AHT target. This information is never sent to us. Clear your browser's site data to remove it. When advertising is shown, Google and its partners may set advertising cookies, described in section 1 and in the Cookie Policy.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. To know more detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers, it can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

4. Third Party Privacy Policies

Agent Desk's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights (EU/UK Visitors)

We would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object to processing, and data portability.

6. CCPA/CPRA Privacy Rights (California Consumers)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California consumers have the right to request information disclosure, deletion, and opt-out of the sale/sharing of personal data.

7. Children's Information

Agent Desk does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this information on our website, please contact us immediately to remove it.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email at jm.me6299@gmail.com.
