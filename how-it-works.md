## The workspace

The [workspace](/portal/) is a single page that holds the three reports and all fifteen tools. A menu on the left groups them under Dashboards, Agent tools, and Travel and lodging. Choosing an item opens it in the main area without reloading the page. On a phone the menu slides in from the left behind a Menu button.

Three controls sit at the bottom of the menu. **Focus mode** hides the title bar and makes the report as tall as the window, which suits a second monitor. **Theme** switches between dark, light and your system's setting, and the workspace starts in dark. The links below them lead to the guides, the contact page and the legal pages. The workspace never shows advertising.

## The reports

The three [Power BI reports](/reports/) are hosted by Microsoft. In the workspace a report opens as soon as you choose it. On its own page it waits for you to press **Load interactive report**, which keeps the page fast. Every report has a reading guide, and each page has links to open the report full screen or in a new tab if your network blocks the embedded view.

## Call Scratchpad

A notepad that saves as you type, with templates for standard, callback and escalation notes and a warning if a note contains something that looks like a card number. Read more on the [Call Scratchpad page](/tools/call-scratchpad/).

## AHT Timer

A stopwatch for one call that tells you in words whether you are on pace against a target. See the [AHT Timer page](/tools/aht-timer/) and the [handle-time guide](/guides/aht-explained/).

## AHT Calculator

Handle time for one call from talk, hold and after-call work, or your average across a shift. See the [AHT Calculator page](/tools/aht-calculator/).

## Phrase Bank

Sixty click-to-copy phrases in twelve categories, including five for lodging and B2B conversations. See the [Phrase Bank page](/tools/phrase-bank/).

## Phonetic Speller

The NATO spelling alphabet for names, emails and references, with a chart. See the [NATO Phonetic Chart page](/tools/nato-phonetic/).

## Text Formatter

Change case, trim spaces and join lines in pasted text, with undo. See the [Text Formatter page](/tools/text-formatter/).

## Time Zones

Convert a callback time into ten zones and see which fall in business hours. See the [Callback Time Zones page](/tools/callback-time-zones/).

## Tools for travel and lodging

Eight tools were built for agents who answer guests and work with hotels, agencies and partners. The [Cancellation Deadline Checker](/tools/cancellation-deadline/) finds the cutoff in the hotel's own time zone. The [Booking Price and Change Calculator](/tools/booking-price/) breaks a stay into room, fees and taxes. The [Commission and Net Rate Calculator](/tools/commission-net-rate/) answers agency questions. The [Group Block and Rooming List](/tools/group-block/) tool sizes a block and checks a rooming list. The [Lodging Case Note Builder](/tools/lodging-case-notes/) structures twelve kinds of case notes. The [Partner SLA Due-Time Calculator](/tools/sla-due-time/) counts response times in business hours. The [Hotel KPI Calculator](/tools/hotel-kpis/) works out occupancy, ADR and RevPAR. The [Guest Name Formatter](/tools/guest-name/) tidies names for reservation systems.

## What stays on your device

Nothing you type is sent to a server. The tools remember your notes, your handle-time target, your case-note drafts, your theme and a few settings in your browser's own storage, and nothing else. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy.html) lists each item. If your browser blocks storage the tools still work, and tell you that they cannot remember anything.

## Questions

::: faq
Q: Do I need an account?
A: No. There is nothing to sign in to.
Q: Does it work offline?
A: The tools work once the page has loaded, but a page you have never opened cannot load offline, and Power BI reports always need a connection.
Q: Which browsers does it support?
A: Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Older browsers may not support every feature, and the tools say so if they cannot start.
:::
