## What to include

A good message makes the problem easy to reproduce. If a tool gave an answer you think is wrong, send the page address, the values you entered and the answer you expected, with your reasoning. If something did not load, say which browser and device you were using and what you saw instead. For a suggestion, describe the job you were trying to do when you wished the tool existed.

## What this address can help with

- Mistakes on a page, in a calculation or in a definition.
- Tools that do not work in your browser.
- Requests for a tool, a guide or a glossary term.
- Questions about how a page or a Power BI report is meant to be read.

## What it cannot help with

Please do not send customer details, booking numbers, card numbers or anything else that identifies a real guest or customer. This address cannot look up accounts or bookings, cannot change anything in a company system, and cannot give legal, financial or tax advice. It also cannot speak for any employer, client or vendor, because the site is independent of all of them. For account-specific questions, use your company's own support route.

## Reporting a problem with a Power BI report

The reports are hosted by Microsoft Power BI. If a report will not open, first try the "Open in a new tab" link on its page, because some networks block the embedded view. If it still fails, include the report name and the message you see when you write.
