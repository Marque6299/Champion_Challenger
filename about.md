## Why it exists

Agents lose time to small jobs that no system quite covers: working out whether a call ran over target, converting a callback time across three time zones, checking whether a cancellation deadline has passed in the hotel's local time, or writing a note that another agent can act on without calling the customer back. Agent Desk collects tools for those jobs in one place, and pairs each tool with a plain explanation so you can see how the answer was reached and judge whether to trust it.

## Who writes it

The site is written and maintained by an operations and reporting analyst who works with contact-centre teams and builds Power BI reports. That background shapes what is here: reports that need explaining, measures that are easy to misread, and tools that save a minute many times a day. Pages carry the date they were last updated, and the tools say what they assume.

## How pages are written and checked

Every guide and tool page is written for this site. Where a page gives a formula, the worked example on that page is calculated with the same code the tool uses, and the code has automated tests that check it against hand-worked figures, including awkward cases such as daylight-saving changes. A page that describes a rule of thumb says so and tells you to check the contract, policy or account instructions that apply to you. Pages do not claim results the site cannot show, and they do not copy text from other sites.

## What this site is not

Agent Desk is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any employer, client or software vendor, and the tools follow no company's procedures. They are aids for working things out, not rulings. Your account's policies, contracts and approved scripts always come first, and the tools never replace the booking, the policy text or a supervisor's instruction. Nothing here is legal, financial or tax advice.

## Privacy by design

The tools run entirely in your browser. They have no sign-in and send nothing you type to any server. Notes and drafts stay in your browser's own storage, and you can delete them at any time. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy.html) and the [cookie policy](/cookie-policy.html) give the full list of what is stored and which third parties are involved.

## Corrections

If you find a wrong figure, a broken tool or a sentence that misleads, please say so on the [contact page](/contact/). Corrections are made on the page itself and the update date changes, so you can see that the page was revised.
