Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Agent Desk uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to recognize you when you visit our website. It details what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We use first-party and third-party cookies for several reasons:

  • Essential Cookies: Technical cookies necessary for the operation of our website, navigation menus, and embedding interfaces.
  • Browser storage (not cookies): The tools keep your call notes and AHT target in local storage on your device. We do not receive this information.
  • Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense): Cookies used to make advertising messages more relevant to you. They perform functions like preventing the same ad from continuously reappearing, ensuring ads are properly displayed, and selecting advertisements based on your interests.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

Third-Party Advertising Cookies:

Google and its partners use advertising cookies to serve personalized ads based on your visit to our portal or other sites across the internet. These cookies do not store personally identifiable information directly, but rely on uniquely identifying your browser and internet device.

  • To manage or opt-out of Google personalized advertising, visit Google Ads Settings.
  • To opt-out of third-party vendor cookies across multiple ad networks, visit Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out Page.

4. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though your access to some functionality and areas may be restricted.

Most browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences through their settings menu:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

5. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please re-visit this Cookie Policy regularly to stay informed about our use of cookies and related technologies.
