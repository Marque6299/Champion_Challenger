About Agent Desk

Agent Desk is a small, independent project: free browser tools for contact center agents, and a home for Power BI reports.

Why it exists

The tools started as small helpers for agents on the floor: a timer that stays accurate, notes with a sensible structure, a quick way to spell an email address aloud. They are shared here for anyone who supports customers for a living.

What is on the site

  • Seven tools that run in your browser: scratchpad, timer, calculator, phrase bank, phonetic speller, text formatter and time zone converter.
  • Power BI reports that summarize metrics for travel locations, products and services.

How it is run

  • No account and no install. What you type stays on your device.
  • Each tool is explained in the how it works guide.
  • Agent Desk is not affiliated with any employer, client or software vendor.
